# Graph Thinking

## Core Principle

Relationships are first-class entities.

Do not immediately solve problems using joins and aggregations.

Ask:

* What are the nodes?
* What are the edges?
* What is the path?
* What is reachable?

## Common Queries

### Connectivity

Can A reach B?

### Traversal

How does A reach B?

### Cost

What is the cheapest path?

### Reachability

What becomes accessible from A?

## Future Features

Potential graph algorithms include:

* Dijkstra
* A*
* K-shortest paths
* Transfer graph traversal
* Accessibility analysis

Design data models so these remain possible.
